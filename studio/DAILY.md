# Daily reel runbook — @myth.scale (what the scheduled task does every day)

Maddy's standing instructions (19 Nov 2026): **one Telugu reel every day, built start to finish and posted automatically at 7:30 PM IST** without waiting for him. He approved auto-posting for these daily reels.

## Style — STORYBOOK 3D (Maddy, 27 Nov 2026: "yes i like it, change every reel in the queue to this format")
Maddy shared his own reference reels (bright 3D storybook, "The Brother Who Never Knew") and approved the demo `stb_bhishma3_te` (Bhishma 3/3). **Every reel now uses this look; the old 2D puppet look is retired.**
- Code: `studio/scenes/stb_storybook_kit.js` (shared kit, auto-prepended by `setup.sh` when a body contains `STORYBOOK`). Template body: `studio/scenes/stb_bhishma3_te_body.js` — `const A = window.STORYBOOK(K, T)`, destructure helpers, build cast with `hero({...})` on the rigged body, one shot per line in `SH`, source pill per line in `SRC`, `return finish({ SH, SRC, SFX })`.
- Look rules: blue sky + puffy cartoon clouds + sun rays, green meadow with grass, rows of round "lollipop" trees, striped camp tents, golden canopy chariots with white horses, soft toon (cel) shading, warm night scenes with torches/diyas. Characters: slim 3D figures with crowns, gold jewellery, patterned dhotis, upper cloth over the left shoulder.
- Cards like the reference: serif (EB Garamond) title at the top of line 1 (story title + "NAME · PART x/3"), small chapter titles ("SHARA SHAYYA — The bed of arrows"), a small dark source pill under the subtitles (e.g. BHEESHMA PARVA), and a dark navy end card with the title in italics + "FOLLOW FOR MORE STORIES".
- Subtitles stay ours (Maddy chose this): Tinglish, 1–2 yellow keywords, Fredoka, capMarginV 520. No watermark, no handle.
- Still vary each reel: the story's own places (river bank, palace hall, forest, night camp, heaven of clouds...), time of day and palette, and a new ending composition. Add new sets / character types to the kit when a story needs them (keep them generic so later reels reuse them).
- Camera: film shots — over-the-shoulder, low angle, close-ups on faces, top-down, slow push-ins; soft cross-fades between lines.
- Queue conversion: every reel queued before 27 Nov 2026 was made in the old 2D look. A queued reel is in the new look only when its `queue/<slug>_te.json` has `"style": "storybook3d"`. **Before building anything else, rebuild the earliest-due queued reels without that field** (same voices in `studio/voices/out/stb_<slug>_te/`, same caption/time), replace the queue `.mp4`/`.jpg`, set `"style": "storybook3d"`, commit + push. If a reel due today is still old-style at 18:45 IST and can't be finished, move it and every later reel back one day rather than posting the old look.

- **Subtitles ON, in Tinglish** (Telugu in English letters, the way people type: "ippatiki", "ela", "ekkada", "Karnudu", "shaapam"). `text` = Tinglish subtitle of exactly what is spoken, 1–2 key words in `*stars*` (turn yellow). Body returns `capFont: 'Fredoka Light', capMarginV: 520, capSize: 64`.

## Series (3 parts, never more)
- Rows in TOPICS.md marked `series: <name> 1/3`, `2/3`, `3/3` are built on 3 consecutive days.
- Part 1 and 2 end with: `మిగతా కథ రేపు Part 2 లో... ఫాలో అవ్వండి!` (Part 3 for part 2). Part 2 and 3 start with one quick recap line ("Ninna choosam…").
- Part 3 ends the story fully and ends with the normal follow line.
- Captions/titles say "Part 1/3" etc. Same series hashtag on all 3 parts.

## Steps
0. **First convert old-style queued reels (see Queue conversion above).** Then: **skip new topics when the queue is full (Maddy, 23 Nov 2026: "generate another 5 videos and schedule it, then you don't need to create again for many days until those scheduled ones are completed").** Count the reels in `queue/*.json` whose `at` is after today. If there are 2 or more, build nothing this run: no reel, no commit, no message to Maddy. Build again only when 1 or 0 future reels are left, so the daily posts never run dry.
1. `git pull`. Take the first `todo` row in `studio/TOPICS.md`. If the topic list has fewer than 5 todo rows, add 10 new good topics (with 1 new 3-part series among them) at the end.
2. Research every fact on the web (WebSearch). Note the source.
3. Write `studio/scenes/stb_<slug>_te_lines.js` (same shape as `stb_karna_te_lines.js`, but `text` = Tinglish subtitle).
4. Voices: build `studio/voices/stb_<slug>_te.json` from `cast.json` (one job per line, voice `maddy2_te2`, speed 1.0, files `01.wav`…) and dispatch `voices.yml` (REST: `gh api -X POST repos/madhureddy18/scraptobuild/actions/workflows/voices.yml/dispatches -f ref=main -f 'inputs[config]=…'`). ~25 min. Collect `voices-out-0/1/2` immediately, trim silence (ffmpeg silenceremove both ends, apad 0.04) into `studio/voices/out/stb_<slug>_te/NN.wav`.
5. Check: dispatch `transcribe.yml` with `model=medium`, `lang=te`. A line that comes back empty or garbled: regenerate it with 2–3 rephrasings (fewer `...`, a comma instead) in one fix run, keep the one that transcribes right, and update that line's `omni`/`text` to the kept wording.
6. Scene: `studio/scenes/stb_<slug>_te_body.js` in the STORYBOOK 3D look (kit + template above), with this story's own sets, palette and ending. Preview locally (`bash studio/render/setup.sh stb_<slug>_te /home/claude/kit` then `cd /home/claude/kit/codevid && SILENT=1 node render.js scenes/stb_<slug>_te.js --preview lines`), tile the stills into one contact sheet and LOOK at it. Fix: characters too small, floating feet, people cut off, empty shots, subtitles covering faces.
7. Render: commit + push, dispatch `render.yml` (`scene=stb_<slug>_te`, `music=studio/music/epic_bed.mp3`). ~25 min. If a shard fails on a network error, re-run failed jobs once. Result is on branch `render-out`.
8. Check the MP4: ffprobe duration, 1080x1920, contact sheet, subtitles visible, no black blocks.
9. Queue: `queue/<slug>_te.mp4`, `.jpg` cover (frame at ~2.5 s), `.json` (Telugu caption with the source, 5 hashtags incl. `mythscale` and one unique tag, first_comment, `"at": "<today>T19:30:00+05:30"`; if it's already past 19:15 IST, use 21:00 today). Never schedule two reels for the same day: use the first evening (19:30, starting today) that has no reel in `queue/` yet — Maddy batches several reels ahead (21 Nov 2026), so this can be days out.
10. Add the reel's row to the Look log above. Mark the topic `done <date>` in TOPICS.md, commit, push (the schedule waiter posts it on time).
11. Send Maddy a short message: topic, the MP4 file, posting time. If something failed and the reel can't be finished well, say so and do NOT queue a half-made reel.
